How to Play

  • Live Rounds (Red): Deal 1 damage if fired at a player
  • Blank Rounds (Gray): Do nothing when fired
  • Items: Can be used to gain advantages during your turn
  • Turn Order: Players take turns firing the shotgun at themselves or the opponent
  • Game End: The game ends when one player's health reaches 0
  • Items include: Magnifying Glass (see next shell), Hand Saw (double damage), Beer (eject current shell), Cigarettes (heal 1 HP)
